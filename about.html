About Ryan

Ryan LaRocca

Founder of LRG Homes. Born and raised in San Jose. Started in the trades, spent years as an agent, and has bought and renovated South Bay houses full-time since 2017.

San Jose, California

The short version

I grew up here. After high school I went to trade school and spent my first working years in commercial HVAC — crawl spaces, rooftops, mechanical rooms. That's where I learned how buildings actually go together, what fails first, and what it really costs to fix.

In 2014 I moved into real estate and worked as an agent, first with Keller Williams and then Intero. I did well at it, but what I liked most was the houses themselves: the ones that needed someone to see past the mess. In 2017 I bought my first investment property. By the time I'd done a few, LRG Homes was my full-time work.

Today that's what the company does: buy single-family homes and small multifamily buildings in the South Bay — often estates, often tired, sometimes a real project — renovate them properly, with permits, and sell them to families who want a finished house.

I studied Business and Economics at San Jose State, which comes in handy. The HVAC years come in handier.
