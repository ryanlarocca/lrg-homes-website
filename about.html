The Team

Meet Our Founders

Real people with deep Bay Area roots, doing honest deals.

RL

Ryan LaRocca

Founder & Director of Acquisitions

Ryan LaRocca is the CEO and Director of Acquisitions at LRG Homes LLC. Raised in San Jose, California, he pursued Business and Economics at San Jose State University. His journey in construction commenced after graduating from vocational school in 2010, leading to a career in commercial HVAC and extensive knowledge of trades and building maintenance.

In 2014, Ryan transitioned into the real estate sector, affiliating with esteemed firms like Keller Williams and Intero Real Estate, earning recognition as a top-performing agent. This role enabled him to grasp the intricacies of enhancing real property and prompted his inaugural investments in 2017. Today, Ryan is a dedicated full-time investor, overseeing 10+ projects annually across residential, multi-family, and commercial real estate domains.

AB

Amanda Bakshi

Co-Founder & Realtor

Amanda was born and raised in San Jose, CA, and attended UC Berkeley, where she graduated with a double major in Political Science and Mass Communications. Her first career was in Property Management where she honed her skills in Marketing & Operations.

She then returned to the South Bay and took a position as a Real Estate Assistant for Ryan LaRocca, and together they have built LRG Homes into the trusted Bay Area investment company it is today. Amanda brings an analytical, client-first approach to every transaction, ensuring both parties come away satisfied.
