How it works

What happens when you call.

One visit. A written cash offer within 24 hours. A closing date you pick — as fast as 5 days, or months out. No repairs, no commissions, no open houses, and nobody pushing you.

1

Reach out

Call, text, or use the form. I'll ask a few questions about the house and your situation. Five minutes, no obligation.

2

I come see it

One visit, at a time that works for you. I'll walk the house, take a look at what it needs, and run the numbers. No open houses, no strangers.

3

Written cash offer within 24 hours

A real number in writing, with the math behind it if you want it. No fees, no commissions, no repairs. The number on the offer is the number you receive.

4

Take your time

Accept it, ask questions, counter, or walk away. If listing with an agent is the better move for you, I'll tell you.

5

Close on your date

As fast as 5 days, or whenever suits you. Escrow handles the money. Need to stay a while after closing? We can arrange that.
