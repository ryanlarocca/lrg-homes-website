Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the process take? ▼
We can have an offer in your hands within 24 hours. Closing typically takes 5–30 days depending on your preference. Need more time to move? No problem — we work on your timeline.
What types of properties do you buy? ▼
Single-family homes, multi-family properties, distressed homes, inherited properties, and probate sales. Condition doesn't matter — we buy as-is.
What areas do you serve? ▼
We buy properties throughout the South Bay and Peninsula — San Jose, Sunnyvale, Santa Clara, Mountain View, Palo Alto, Redwood City, San Mateo, and surrounding communities.
Do I need to make repairs first? ▼
Absolutely not. We buy homes in any condition — cosmetic issues, structural problems, major updates needed. We handle all of it after closing.
Are there any fees or commissions? ▼
None. Our offer is all-in. No agent commissions, no hidden closing costs, no surprise fees. What we offer is what you receive.
What if I need to stay in the home after closing? ▼
We can arrange a rent-back so you can stay in the home after closing while you find your next place. Just let us know your situation and we'll find a solution.
