The story behind the house

This was a family home, held in their mother's trust. Mark and his brother wanted it sold quickly and as-is, without fixing anything first. We wrote the offer, the trust's attorney reviewed it, and we closed in under a month.

Then the house got everything it needed: foundation and pier repair, a new electrical panel, new windows, a new kitchen, both bathrooms redone, new floors, a rebuilt staircase, and fresh paint inside and out, all permitted and inspected by the City of San Jose.
