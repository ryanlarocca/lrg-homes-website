Estate sale · Blossom Valley, San Jose · Closed October 2026

A family's estate, settled from out of state

Rob and Diana live a long way from San Jose. They'd inherited a 1971 three-bedroom in Blossom Valley that needed updating, and they didn't want to run a remodel and a listing from a thousand miles away.

We agreed on a price, I did my inspection quickly, the paperwork went over by DocuSign, and we opened escrow at Chicago Title. I called them most weeks until we closed on October 1st. Then my crew gave the house a light refresh — floors, paint, a cleaned-up yard — and it went on the market.

Who you're dealing with

Local, hands-on, and not going anywhere.

I grew up in San Jose. I went to trade school and spent my first working years in commercial HVAC, which is where I learned how buildings actually go together — and what it costs when they don't. In 2014 I got into real estate, working as an agent with Keller Williams and then Intero, and in 2017 I bought my first investment property. Since then LRG Homes has been my full-time work: buying houses that need help, fixing them properly, and selling them to families.

I keep the operation small on purpose. When you call, you get me. When we agree on a number, that's the number.

How a sale works

Three steps, no surprises.

1

Tell me about the house

Call, text, or use the form below. I'll ask a few questions and set a time to come see it — one visit, no open houses.

2

Get a written cash offer within 24 hours

After I've seen the house. No fees, no commissions, no repairs. The number on the offer is the number you receive.

3

Close when it suits you

As fast as 5 days, or months out if you need the time. If you need to stay a while after closing, we can arrange that too.
